Affiliated Federations Directory

Official mountain climbing clubs, rescue teams, and alpine federations around the world.

Alpine Club of Pakistan (ACP) Est. 1974
Islamabad, Pakistan | Karakoram & Western Himalayas

The national mountain club of Pakistan. They coordinate climbing permits, porter safety, and helicopter rescues on K2, Nanga Parbat, and the Karakoram peaks.

The Alpine Club Est. 1857
London, United Kingdom | International Mountain Climbing Club

Founded in 1857 as the world's first mountaineering club. They keep historical climbing journals, support exploration of remote peaks, and help new climbers learn safety skills.

American Alpine Club (AAC) Est. 1902
Golden, Colorado, USA | Alaska Range & Northern Cordillera

Publishes the annual American Alpine Journal and mountain safety reports. They protect public climbing areas, care for wilderness lands, and support mountain rescue.

The Himalayan Club Est. 1928
Mumbai, India | Greater Himalayan Range & Karakoram

Formed in 1928 to encourage mountain exploration across the Himalayas. They record mountain history and celebrate the achievements of local Sherpa and Ladakhi guides.

Club Alpin Français (FFCAM) Est. 1874
Paris, France | European Alps & Pyrenees

Manages more than 120 mountain huts across the French Alps, trains mountain guides, and leads cleanup projects to keep alpine trails clean.

Swiss Alpine Club (SAC) Est. 1863
Bern, Switzerland | Pennine & Bernese Alps

Pioneers in high-altitude mountain huts, detailed trail maps, and air rescue coordination across famous Swiss peaks like the Matterhorn and Eiger.

Club Alpino Italiano (CAI) Est. 1863
Milan, Italy | Dolomites & Gran Paradiso

Founded in 1863, CAI organized the historic first climb of K2 in 1954. Today they care for thousands of kilometers of mountain trails and shelters across the Italian Alps.

Japan Mountaineering & Sport Climbing Association Est. 1905
Tokyo, Japan | Japanese Northern & Southern Alps

Coordinates mountain climbing throughout Japan and trains climbers for big expeditions to the highest peaks of the Himalayas and Karakoram.

Alpine Ascents helps climbers plan safe expeditions to the world's highest mountains. We focus on skilled guide work, clean mountain camps, and caring for local climbing staff.
