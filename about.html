Leave No Trace & Clean Climbing

We carry all trash and empty oxygen bottles down from every high camp. Following clean climbing rules, our guides use removable safety gear instead of drilling permanent metal bolts into natural rock.

Sherpa Porter Welfare Protocol

All our Sherpa team members receive full health and rescue insurance. We provide them with the same warm down suits as our lead guides, along with fair bonus pay for every summit trip.

Maximum load ceilings: 20kg per porter.
Mandatory supplemental O₂ provided above 7,000m.
Family educational endowment funded per summit expedition.

Alpine Ascents helps climbers plan safe expeditions to the world's highest mountains. We focus on skilled guide work, clean mountain camps, and caring for local climbing staff.
