K2 Winter 2021 16 January 2021 | 17:00 PKT

Historic First Winter Ascent of K2 at -60°C

On 16 January 2021, ten brave Nepali mountaineers made history by reaching the summit of K2 in winter for the first time. In freezing temperatures below -60°C, the team waited 10 meters below the crest to step onto the summit shoulder arm-in-arm while singing Nepal's national anthem.

Summit Leaders: Nirmal Purja, Mingma G, Mingma David Sherpa, Gelje Sherpa.
Project Possible April - October 2019

All 14 Eight-Thousanders in 189 Days

Breaking the previous record of over 7 years, Nepali climber Nirmal Purja and his team climbed all 14 peaks above 8,000 meters in just 6 months, completing one of the greatest achievements in modern mountain climbing history.

Pace: 14 Summits completed across 3 countries in 6 months and 6 days.
High Altitude Rescue July 2023 | 7,600m

Broad Peak Night Crevasse Extraction

During a freezing night storm near 7,600 meters on Broad Peak, a solo climber fell into a hidden 15-meter crevasse. Our Sherpa team set up snow anchors in heavy wind, lowered rescue ropes, and pulled the cold climber back to safety in less than 90 minutes.

Outcome: 100% survival, zero frostbite amputations, safe helicopter evacuation.
Alpine Style Climb October 2024

Ama Dablam SW Ridge in 14-Hour Push

A pair of climbers went from Base Camp to the top of Ama Dablam and back in just 14 hours. By moving fast and skipping overnight camps, they climbed with minimal gear, carrying only a single thin rope, ice screws, and energy food.

Style: Pure single-push alpine style with 100% gear pack-out.
